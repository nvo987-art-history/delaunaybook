
# Delaunay Book

**Color as a Generative Principle in the Work of Sonia Delaunay**

Nicholas Van-Orton · 2026

## Overview

Delaunay Book is the digital publication website for a scholarly study of
colour as a generative and organising principle in the work of Sonia Delaunay.

The study follows a historical and conceptual trajectory from:

1. scientific colour theory;
2. modern theories of perception;
3. Simultanism and Sonia Delaunay;
4. textile and material systems;
5. digital imaging;
6. algorithmic and generative art.
