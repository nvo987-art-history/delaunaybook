# Support

## General Support

For questions concerning the Delaunay Book website, publication metadata,
bibliographic information or technical resources, contact:

contact@delaunaybook.us

## Before Contacting Support

For publication-related questions, please provide:

- the title of the edition;
- ISBN or DOI, if applicable;
- the relevant page or URL;
- a concise description of the issue.

For technical issues, please provide:

- the affected URL;
- browser and operating system, if relevant;
- the approximate date and time;
- reproduction steps;
- screenshots or error messages where useful.
