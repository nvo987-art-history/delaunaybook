# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project is intended to support scholarly, cultural and technical work
related to the Delaunay Book and its associated digital publication resources.

Participants are expected to contribute in a professional, respectful and
evidence-oriented manner.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully;
- distinguish documented facts from interpretation and speculation;
- provide sources when making substantive factual claims;
- respect intellectual property and attribution;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and personal attacks;
- report technical or security problems through the appropriate channel;
- avoid intentionally misleading metadata or identity claims.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- harassment or threats;
- discriminatory or hateful conduct;
- deliberate impersonation;
- malicious alteration of publication metadata;
- deliberate dissemination of fabricated citations or identifiers;
- publishing private information without authorization;
- attempts to compromise or disrupt the website or its infrastructure.

## Scholarly Conduct

Disagreement concerning interpretation, methodology or historical claims is
welcome. Criticism should address the argument, evidence or methodology rather
than the person.

## Enforcement

Concerns regarding conduct may be reported to:

contact@delaunaybook.us

Reports should include enough information to allow the issue to be understood
and investigated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to interactions directly associated with the
Delaunay Book project, including its website, metadata, documentation and
project communication channels.
