## Publication

The English print edition is:

**Color as a Generative Principle in the Work of Sonia Delaunay**

ISBN: 978-615-83133-5-3

DOI: https://doi.org/10.17613/802rd-bjr69

The publication studies colour as a structural and generative principle,
connecting scientific colour theory, perception, modern painting, Sonia
Delaunay's Simultanism, textile design, digital imaging and generative art.
